About

A global buying assistant for physical-game collectors.

RetroNomad is an independent software project in early development. Its goal is to identify exact release characteristics before comparing marketplace listings.

Launch focus

UK/PAL PlayStation 1, PlayStation 2 and Dreamcast releases.

Core principle

Prefer a transparent “needs review” result over an unsupported classification.

Global direction

Expand to North American, Japanese and additional console/PC physical releases over time.

PALScout is RetroNomad's first regional identification engine, focused on UK and European PAL releases.
